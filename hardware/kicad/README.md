`kicad-cli pcb drc`, with Gerbers, drill, BOM, pick-and-place and STEP exported by `kicad-cli`.
On the development laptop KiCad 10.0.3 is installed; `kicad-cli` is at
`"/c/Program Files/KiCad/10.0/bin/kicad-cli.exe"` (not on PATH).
